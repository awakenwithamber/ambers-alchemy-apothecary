## LIVE REALITY AUDIT
- The site is a single static SPA (`index.html` + `app.js` + data scripts) published from the repo root. `grimior.html` is a separate Living Grimoire page.
- Before this run, the cart and checkout called `/api/*` routes that exist only as Vercel handlers (`api/*.js`, `server.js`). On Netlify they fell through to the SPA catch-all, so Stripe card checkout could not work.
- Two carts ran at the same time: `app.js` and `js/cart.js`. `js/cart.js` auto-wired every "Add to Cart" button with a $12.99 fallback price, which caused double adds. `js/form-handlers.js` also hijacked the checkout form.
- Order "paid" state was decided in the browser. The client added a flat 8% tax. `FREESHIP1` was published in client code.
- The page claimed fabricated social proof ("Best Sellers", "Trusted by hundreds", "Fan Favorite", "Customer Favorite") and a hardcoded herb count of 316/306.
- The page used 432 Hz claims.
- Retired terms were live: age reversal, miracle hair regrowth, parasite cleanse, heavy metal detox, diabetic support, blood sugar.
- `custom-creations.js` and `guided-flow.js` were referenced but missing (404s). `herbal-ai-chat.js` called a Vercel-only AI route.
