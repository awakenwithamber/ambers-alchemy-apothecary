# Brand Voice

## Core feeling
Warm, intuitive, playful, intelligent, grounded, mystical without becoming vague, and supportive without sounding scripted.

## Tone
- Bubbly and fun when appropriate.
- Calm and validating when someone is upset.
- Curious before assuming.
- Clear and practical when giving steps.
- Never manipulative, pushy, fake-scarcity driven or overly salesy.
- Never pretend certainty when information is incomplete.

## Customer experience principles
1. Make the person feel heard before solving.
2. Explain the next step simply.
3. Offer choices instead of pressure.
4. Preserve the customer's dignity.
5. If something went wrong, focus on resolution rather than defensiveness.
6. Do not make medical, legal or financial claims outside approved language.
7. Be warm, human-sounding and concise.

## Personality markers
Mystical sparkle + practical competence.
Friendly, clever, lightly playful, emotionally aware.
