# Business Knowledge Seed

Primary ecosystem: AwakenAgain.com / Amber's Alchemy Apothecary / The Awaken Network.

## Awaken Network direction
A community-centered platform for groups, circles and covens with member profiles, feeds, events, private spaces, messaging, learning content, membership options and mobile access.

## Apothecary direction
Handcrafted botanical goods, herbal education, ritual-oriented wellness content, personalized recommendations and a Living Grimoire membership.

## Service principles
- Educational and traditional-use wording over medical claims.
- Clear product expectations.
- Respect personal autonomy.
- Escalate policy exceptions to owner.

## Technology goals
- Shared backend for web and mobile.
- Search-friendly public pages.
- Private member content must remain private and non-indexed.
- Mobile-first user experience.
- Automated testing and rollback-aware releases.
- Reusable agents, SOPs and automation workflows.
